# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

**Primary:** Shippers and logistics customers who need to:
- Track shipments in real-time
- Get shipping quotes quickly
- Manage business accounts with volume discounts

**Secondary:** Business customers needing enterprise logistics solutions and tariff advisory information

## Product Purpose

OPS Logistics provides global shipping and logistics services with an emphasis on transparency, reliability, and expert guidance through tariff changes. The platform enables customers to track shipments, request quotes, ship items, and access tariff advisory services.

Success means: fast shipment tracking, intuitive quote requests, clear value proposition for business accounts, and positioning as a trusted tariff-aware logistics partner.

## Positioning

Distinct from generic carriers: OPS combines real-time tracking, expert tariff navigation, and personalized business account support. The brand promise is "Delivering Excellence Worldwide."

## Constraints

- Must support international shipping context
- Tariff information must be current and accessible
- Live tracking capability required
- Mobile-first responsive design (significant mobile traffic expected)
- Professional B2B credibility required

## Key Features

1. **Real-time tracking** — search by tracking number
2. **Shipping quotes** — cost estimation tool
3. **Business accounts** — for regular shippers with volume discounts
4. **Tariff advisory** — navigating trade complexity
5. **Customer portal** — for registered users
