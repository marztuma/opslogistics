Three stages, and they are not about size

A volume agreement scales with you. What matters is pattern and predictability, not the absolute number of shipments or the weight per month.

Stage 01

First shipments abroad

  • ✓ Occasional exports, no pattern yet
  • ✓ Quote each shipment individually
  • ✓ See if a lane repeats itself
  • ✓ Price at the counter, or negotiate
Talk through it
Stage 02

Shipping regularly

  • ✓ Pattern has appeared, lanes repeat
  • ✓ Volume is predictable now
  • ✓ Standard monthly invoicing makes sense
  • ✓ Pricing per-lane and annual, not per-shipment
Open an account
Stage 03

Multi-market and scaling

  • ✓ Multiple markets, trading partners expect good rates
  • ✓ Volume is large and diversified now
  • ✓ Dedicated contact and account management
  • ✓ Custom solutions on tailored terms
Explore Supply Chain

Do these sound wildly on-size to you? Skip a stage. A six-month look at what you are actually moving — lanes, volumes, weights — will tell you which stage fits. If the pattern does not yet exist it is not time yet, and we would rather say so than sign you into an arrangement that does not feel like it is saving you money or time.
