What gets measured, and what it means

Everything. You see the metrics that matter to your business, in real time.

First-pass accuracy

The percentage of orders shipped 100% correctly the first time. Tells you how much rework you are paying for.

On-time dispatch

What percentage of orders shipped on the date promised. Shows whether your customers get what they expect.

Inventory accuracy

Physical count versus system count. Shrinkage and misplacement show up here, as does the cost of poor discipline.

Return and rework

The percentage of orders that come back or need correction. This is directly tied to your customer satisfaction.

Staffing & efficiency

Orders per labour hour, turnover rates, absenteeism. Shows whether the operation is efficient and stable.
