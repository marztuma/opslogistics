What your cargo travels in

The container matters. Size, type and packaging affect price, speed, handling and what can travel alongside your cargo.

20ft Container

Standard single box. 20 cubic meters, roughly 18 tons.

40ft Container

Double the space. 40 cubic meters, roughly 28 tons.

40ft HC

High-cube. Same footprint, extra height. Space, not weight.

How freight is actually priced

Not a mystery, not a guess. Base cost plus adjustments. It is what you see in a quote that you negotiate from.

What moves the base rate

  • Origin and destination

    Where it starts and where it ends. Infrastructure and demand matter.

  • Weight and volume

    You pay for both. Whichever consumes more of the truck/ship.

  • Container type

    Reefer costs more than dry. High-cube costs more than standard.

  • Frequency

    Regular lanes have better rates. One-time shipments cost more.

Adjustments and surcharges

  • Hazmat, batteries, dangerous goods

    Handling and paperwork have a cost. Usually 10-30% of freight.

  • Fuel and currency

    Fuel surcharge. Currency surcharge. Market-linked adjustments.

  • Customs and documentation

    Entry, declaration, licensing fees. Usually flat or per-doc.

  • Insurance and liability

    Optional for shipments, standard for high-value cargo.
