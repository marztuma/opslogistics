Fashion returns are not an exception, they are the model

Customers buy three sizes to keep one. That is not about the return policy. It is the policy working—and it means a shipment that goes forward and one that comes back. Every unit of those returns has economic value or becomes a liability.

Bulk carries from the supplier

Bulk warehousing and consolidation reduces logistics cost per unit.

Unsoldable stock

Handling costs

Refund timeline

Faster returns processing means faster customer refunds and lower disputes.

Send us your SKU count, not your carton count.

Retail and fashion logistics is unit-based, not box-based. We build a logistics flow around SKU velocity, shelf life, and destination priority. That tells us whether the cost problem is at the warehouse or in the network.
