Five layers between your product and e-write-off

Healthcare product failure in transit becomes a financial liability and a patient safety issue.

Documentation & audit trail

+

Every movement, every temperature reading, every handoff is documented. Regulators expect full traceability. Missing a single entry makes the entire shipment suspect.

Temperature & environmental seals

+

Data loggers and environmental monitors track conditions in real-time. Excursions outside acceptable range trigger immediate notification and investigation protocols.

Packaging & protective measures

+

Specialized packaging with phase change materials, insulation, and shock protection is non-negotiable. Every shipment gets customized to its product profile and transit duration.

Insurance & liability coverage

+

Healthcare shipments require specialized insurance that covers regulatory violations, patient harm, and recall costs. Standard cargo insurance is insufficient.

The product itself

+

If all layers work and the product still fails to perform, the shipment is a total loss. Your capital, reputation, and potentially patient outcomes are at risk.

The only supply chain that has to come back

Healthcare logistics is unique. Products used in patient care cannot be disposed of like standard goods. They must be returned, tracked, and either refurbished or properly destroyed.

Reverse logistics program

Every patient-facing product generates a reverse logistics obligation. OPS manages the return, inspection, and processing.

Refurbishment & recertification

Returned products are assessed, refurbished if possible, and recertified for reuse. Waste minimization protects margins.

Biohazard disposal

Products that cannot be reused are incinerated or disposed through regulated medical waste channels. Full documentation is retained.

Stock that circulates instead of selling

Healthcare inventory does not sit in warehouses. It circulates between hospitals, clinics, field teams, and patients. OPS manages this multi-leg logistics flow to ensure stock is available where it is needed, when it is needed.

Hub & spoke distribution

Centralized warehousing feeds regional distribution centers that supply local medical facilities and field sites.

Real-time visibility

Every unit is tracked through the supply chain. Inventory data feeds back to your planning systems for demand forecasting.
