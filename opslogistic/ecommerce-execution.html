Everything in fulfillment is a deadline

Most online retailers commit to a delivery date when the customer clicks Buy. We have hours. The clock starts when the order lands with us. The critical path is fixed.

15:00

Order arrives. Received, logged, queued for picking.

15:20

Pick list printed. Picker has the location and the item.

16:40

Packed and labelled. Ready for the carrier.

17:00

Handed to carrier. In the vehicle before it leaves.

Next day

Delivered. Customer is happy or you refund and retry.

Miss any of those windows and the customer's commitment is broken. Miss all of them and the customer's retailer takes the blame.

How orders reach us, and data goes back

The connection is the backbone. Most retailers use one of four methods.

1

API integration

Real-time orders and live inventory. The integration your systems speak directly.

2

File transfer

Scheduled daily loads. A batch process for businesses still on file-based systems.

3

Portal entry

Manual input through our web portal. For smaller volumes or occasional shipments.

4

EDI integration

Enterprise standard. For large retailers with existing EDI infrastructure.

All four paths connect you to one system. The integration you choose depends on your size, existing tech stack, and how much control you want over the process.
