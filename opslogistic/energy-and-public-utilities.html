What it travels on

Energy cargo uses every transport mode. The selection depends on weight, size, route, and project timeline. Often, multiple modes combine in one shipment.

Heavylift & RoRo vessels

Specialized ships designed to carry oversized and heavy equipment. RoRo vessels for rolling cargo, heavylift cranes for extreme loads.

Barge & inland waterway

When land routes are too congested or terrain is impassable, barge transport via rivers and canals can move massive loads efficiently.

Heavy trucks & rail

Over-the-road and rail transport for inland movement. Requires specialized trailers, route surveys, and sometimes temporary infrastructure.

The last two kilometres

The final leg of energy logistics is the most complex. Site access, terrain conditions, equipment placement, and on-site coordination determine whether a project succeeds on schedule. OPS coordinates with the project team to ensure seamless handoff.

We have managed final delivery on remote mining sites, desert facilities, and complex urban locations. The common denominator is understanding site constraints before moving the cargo.
