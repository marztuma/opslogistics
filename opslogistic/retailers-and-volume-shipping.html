01 – THE DISPATCH

One dispatch day, two sets of hands

One team picking and one team getting ready on the other end. Everything that leaves you in the morning gets sorted and ready to hand over to you in the afternoon.

Up to the loading door

  • ✓ Collection call to confirm time
  • ✓ Pickup within your window
  • ✓ Labels
  • ✓ Manifest signed
  • ✓ Tracking live

From the door onward

  • ✓ Collections
  • ✓ Sort and breakdown
  • ✓ Customs
  • ✓ Pallets and consolidation
  • ✓ Delivery appointment
The volume shipment is consolidated, not drop-speeded. If you are sending three pallets a week and one shipper sends one a month, yours still goes full, but theirs is held for a window to pack. It is how we can make smaller volumes look like real prices.
02 – THE COLLECTION

Four ways parcels leave your building

Pick the method that works for your space and your schedule. Everything is logged, labeled and live-tracked from the moment it leaves your dock.

Scheduled daily collection

A regular time, same day every week. You load, we load, tracking starts.

On-demand collection

You call before noon, we collect before five. The fee is higher, but sometimes higher is exactly what the week needs.

Standing collection order

Three pallets every Tuesday, automatically. No calls, no thinking. Billing follows automatically.

Drop off

Bring it to the terminal. Cheapest option. Tracking picks up the moment you hand it over.

03 – PRICING

Five things that move a parcel rate

Transparency means you can see what is being priced. Discount for consolidation, markup for hazmat, adjustment for fuel — everything is itemized.

Base rate

Where from, where to, how often. The core cost.

Consolidation discount

Multi-volume shipments pack more efficiently. The discount is real.

Address quality

Bad addresses cost rework. Good ones are faster and cheaper to deliver.

Where your customers are

A customer in a capital city gets better rates than one at the end of a rural road, even on the same route.

Seasonal mix

January looks different from July. Your rates are priced across the year, not the month you signed.

04 – GETTING STARTED

The conversation to have in August, not November

Early conversations mean we can stage a network to suit your shipment pattern. Late conversations mean we fit you into what we have. The price is not the difference.

Starting early

If you are thinking about switching, a conversation in Q3 means we can make sure your network is ready for Q4 and peak season.

Call early, test on one lane, then gradually move volume.

Mid-year check

If your current setup is not keeping pace with growth, August is the time to know. We can adjust volumes and routes before the rush.

New lanes take time to set up right.

Why August matters

Wait until November and the network is full. Space is allocated. Routes are running at capacity. We can still do it, but you will not get what the early birds do.

Three months lets us stage correctly.
